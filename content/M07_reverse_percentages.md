# M07 — Reverse percentages

**Status: Draft — awaiting review. Not approved for PowerPoint.**

## Scope

Recover the original amount. Identify which percentage the given amount represents before calculating. Depth also separates an added fixed charge.

Challenge progression: known percentage of a whole → undo a percentage change → remove a fixed charge and undo the change.

Calculator proposal: calculator allowed; the objective is identifying the original amount, not long division.

## Slide 1 — Worked demo

Instruction: **Follow each step down your column.**

| Row | Start | Build | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Full Question | 25% of an amount is £20. Find the whole amount. | A price after a 20% discount is £64. Find the original price. | An item is discounted by 25%, then £10 delivery is added. The bill is £70. Find the original item price. |
| Keywords & Calculation | £20 represents 25%, not 100%. | £64 represents 80% of the original. | The bill includes delivery. The discounted item represents 75%. |
| Written Method | £20 ÷ 25 = £0.80 per 1%; £0.80 × 100 = £80. | £64 ÷ 80 × 100 = £80. | £70 − £10 = £60; £60 ÷ 75 × 100 = £80. |
| Ballpark Check & Math | 25% of £80 = £20. | 20% of £80 is £16; £80 − £16 = £64. | 75% of £80 is £60; £60 + £10 = £70. |
| Final Answer | £80. | £80. | £80. |

### Diagram / layout specification

Use editable percentage bars labelled 25% = £20 and 100% = ?. For Core label 80% = £64 and 100% = ?. For Depth show delivery as a separate £10 box outside the percentage bar; it is not part of the original item price.

Instruction: **Complete each step in your booklet or webapp.** Use the row prompts above.

Teacher working:

- T1: 32÷40×100 = **£80**.
- T2: 99 represents 110%; 99÷110×100 = **£90**.
- T3: 72−8=64; 64÷80×100 = **£80**.

## Checks and review points

- Do not reverse a 20% discount by adding 20% to the sale price.
- Apply and undo fixed charges in the stated order.
- Check every answer by running the original change forwards.

- Seven slides; 72 distinct independent questions, plus the worked demo and two practice grids.
- Keep these exact values when creating slides. Recheck readability before publishing.
- The proposed challenge progression, calculator policy and row wording require user review.
