# M08 — Missing lengths from area and volume

**Status: Draft — awaiting review. Not approved for PowerPoint.**

## Scope

Work backwards from an area or volume. Use the correct formula, distinguish perpendicular height from a sloping side, and make units consistent. Restrict shapes to rectangles, triangles, parallelograms and cuboids.

Challenge progression: rectangle → triangle or parallelogram → cuboid, including mixed length units.

Calculator proposal: no calculator for the chosen values.

## Slide 1 — Worked demo

Instruction: **Follow each step down your column.**

| Row | Thread 1: Guided | Thread 2: Core | Thread 3: Depth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Full Question | A rectangle has area 48 cm² and length 8 cm. Find its width. | A triangle has area 30 cm² and base 10 cm. Find its perpendicular height. | A cuboid has volume 240 cm³, width 40 mm and height 6 cm. Find its length in cm. |
| Keywords & Calculation | Area = length × width; 48 = 8 × width. | Area = base × height ÷ 2; 30 = 10 × height ÷ 2. | 40 mm = 4 cm. Volume = length × width × height. |
| Written Method | 48 ÷ 8 = 6. | 30 × 2 ÷ 10 = 6. | 240 ÷ (4 × 6) = 10. |
| Ballpark Check & Math | 8 × 6 = 48 cm². | 10 × 6 ÷ 2 = 30 cm². | 10 × 4 × 6 = 240 cm³. |
| Final Answer | 6 cm. | 6 cm. | 10 cm. |

### Diagram / layout specification

Provide editable diagrams for all three demo shapes. Label the rectangle 8 cm and width ?. On the triangle show a 10 cm base and a dashed perpendicular height with a right-angle marker. On the cuboid label 40 mm, 6 cm and length ?. Put the area/volume beside the shape. Mark all drawings “Not to scale”. Practice questions are self-contained without measuring a diagram.

## Checks and review points

- Final answers are lengths (cm), not cm² or cm³.
- Multiplying the two known cuboid dimensions is one step before division.
- Triangle questions always ask for perpendicular height.
- This single module mixes shape formulas; review whether it should be split into area and volume for this group.

- Seven slides; 72 distinct independent questions, plus the worked demo and two practice grids.
- Keep these exact values when creating slides. Recheck readability before publishing.
- The proposed challenge progression, calculator policy and row wording require user review.
