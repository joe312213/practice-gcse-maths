# M09 — Best buys and unit costs

**Status: Draft — awaiting review. Not approved for PowerPoint.**

## Scope

Compare the same quantities, then distinguish the lowest unit price from the cheapest whole-pack purchase for a required quantity. Depth explicitly excludes mixing pack types.

Challenge progression: cost per item → compare unit costs → compare whole-pack purchase costs.

Calculator proposal: calculator allowed; show the calculations and decisions.

## Row labels and practice prompts

Use these exact labels and prompts on both step-by-step practice slides. Repeat the same prompt across columns unless a genuine difference in the task requires a change.

| Row label | Student prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Keywords & Calculation | Identify the keywords. Write down the calculation(s) you need to do. |

| Ballpark Check & Math | Check your answer using rough ballpark calculations. Does it make sense? |
| Final Answer | Write your final answer to the question. Include the correct units. |

## Slide 1 — Worked demo

Instruction: **Follow each step down your column.**

| Row | Start | Build | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Full Question | Six pens cost £3. Find the cost per pen. | Pack A has 6 pens for £3. Pack B has 10 pens for £4.50. Which has the lower cost per pen? | You need at least 12 pens. A packs hold 6 for £3; B packs hold 10 for £4.50. Buy one pack type only. Which costs less? |
| Keywords & Calculation | Find the cost of one pen. | Compare costs for the same quantity: one pen. | Compare whole-pack costs for at least 12 pens. |
| Written Method | £3 ÷ 6 = £0.50. | A: £3÷6 = £0.50. B: £4.50÷10 = £0.45. | A: 2 packs cost £6. B: 2 packs cost £9. |
| Ballpark Check & Math | 6 × £0.50 = £3. | Ten pens at A’s rate would cost £5, more than £4.50. | Both choices supply at least 12. The cheaper unit price does not guarantee the cheaper purchase. |
| Final Answer | 50p per pen. | Pack B: 45p per pen. | Buy 2 A packs for £6. |

### Diagram / layout specification

Use editable pack cards with number of items and price. Label each clearly A or B. On the Depth demo show two A packs supplying 12 pens and two B packs supplying 20 pens. Do not use relative picture size to suggest value.

## Checks and review points

- Questions state “at least” and “one pack type only” to make the decision unambiguous.
- Calculate whole packs by rounding up, never to nearest.
- All prices are exact pence. No ties occur in the independent comparisons.
- Depth is about satisfying demand, not merely repeating the Core unit-price calculation.

- Seven slides; 72 distinct independent questions, plus the worked demo and two practice grids.
- Keep these exact values when creating slides. Recheck readability before publishing.
- The proposed challenge progression, calculator policy and row wording require user review.
