# M12 — Speed distance and time

**Status: Draft — awaiting review. Not approved for PowerPoint.**

## Scope

Select the required calculation, convert minutes to hours, and use total distance divided by total elapsed time for average speed. Include stops only when explicitly stated.

Challenge progression: one calculation in matching units → minutes/hours conversion → a whole-journey average.

Calculator proposal: calculator allowed. Where needed, give speeds to 2 decimal places; keep unrounded working.

Use these exact labels and prompts on both step-by-step practice slides. Repeat the same prompt across columns unless a genuine difference in the task requires a change.

## Slide 1 — Worked demo

Instruction: **Follow each step down your column.**

| Row | Start | Build | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Full Question | A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. Find its average speed. | A car travels 90 km in 1 hour 30 minutes. Find its average speed. | A car travels 48 km in 1 hour, then 30 km in 30 minutes. Find its average speed over the whole journey. |
| Keywords & Calculation | Speed = distance ÷ time. | 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 hours. | Use total distance and total time. |
| Written Method | 120 ÷ 2 = 60. | 90 ÷ 1.5 = 60. | 48+30=78 km; 1+0.5=1.5 h; 78÷1.5=52. |
| Ballpark Check & Math | 60 km each hour for 2 hours gives 120 km. | 60×1.5 = 90 km. Do not use 1.30 hours. | First-leg speed 48, second-leg speed 60. The result 52 lies between them. |
| Final Answer | 60 km/h. | 60 km/h. | 52 km/h. |

## Checks and review points

- Keep km, hours and km/h consistent. No miles-to-kilometres conversion is assumed.
- Independent Depth questions have terminating answers; practice slide 2 includes a repeating result, with rounding explicitly allowed.
- Average speed is not generally the mean of the leg speeds.
- Review whether m/s and seconds should be a later extension rather than increasing this module’s scope.

- Seven slides; 72 distinct independent questions, plus the worked demo and two practice grids.
- Keep these exact values when creating slides. Recheck readability before publishing.
- The proposed challenge progression, calculator policy and row wording require user review.
