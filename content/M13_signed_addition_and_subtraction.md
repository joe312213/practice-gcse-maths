# M13 — Signed numbers: addition and subtraction

**Status: PowerPoint review draft authorised by the user on 20 September 2026. Content remains open to revision.**

Purpose: strengthen arithmetic needed for Foundation GCSE questions and later algebra. No calculator. The two signed-number sets are proposed as groundwork before the remaining modules; file IDs do not prescribe teaching order.

Start: add or subtract a positive number, including crossing zero. Build: add or subtract a negative number. Confidence: combine two additions/subtractions, keeping each number’s sign.

Adding a positive number moves right; adding a negative number moves left. Subtraction means adding the opposite: a − b = a + (−b). This includes subtracting a negative number. Do not use “two minuses make a plus” as a rule for every calculation.

Seven teaching/question slides in the teaching deck; six answer sections in the single separate answer deck shared by all topics. S01 is a worked demo; S02–S07 each have an answer partner, labelled S02-A through S07-A. The answer sections below are content specifications for that shared deck, not slides to interleave into the teaching deck. Show them after students have attempted the questions. The four banks provide repeated starters, not one sitting. Students can begin with the suitable thread; the three threads are not GCSE grade labels.

## Rules recap — immediately after the initial assessment

A dedicated guide slide precedes the worked demo so that the rules and examples remain readable. Existing question content and stable S01–S07 references are unchanged. This gives nine physical question-deck slides.

Title: **Signed numbers: addition and subtraction**
Subtitle: **Rules recap • Start at the first number.**

| Rule | What to do | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a positive number | Move right: the value increases. | −4 + 7 = 3 |
| Add a negative number | Move left: the value decreases. | 4 + (−7) = 4 − 7 = −3 |
| Subtract a positive number | Move left: the value decreases. | 3 − 8 = −5 |
| Subtract a negative number | Move right: subtracting a negative is adding a positive. | 6 − (−3) = 6 + 3 = 9 |

Visible explanation: **In 6 − (−3), the first − means subtract; the − inside brackets belongs to the number −3.**

Visible general rule: **To subtract a number, add its opposite. Keep the first number unchanged.**

Visible caution with contrast: **Two negative numbers do not automatically give a positive answer: −4 + (−3) = −7.**

These are addition/subtraction rules. Do not import the multiplication/division “same signs/different signs” shortcut into this set. The next slide retains the complete worked grid and editable number lines.

| Check the Answer | 3 − 7 = −4. Answer: 3. | 9 + (−3) = 6. Answer: 9. | 5 + (−2) = 3; 3 − 8 = −5. Answer: 5. |
