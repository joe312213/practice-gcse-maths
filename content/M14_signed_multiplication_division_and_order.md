# M14 — Signed numbers: multiplication, division and order of operations

**Status: Draft — exact content for review; not approved for PowerPoint.**

Purpose: strengthen arithmetic needed for Foundation GCSE questions and later algebra. No calculator. The two signed-number sets are proposed as groundwork before the remaining modules; file IDs do not prescribe teaching order.

Start: multiply or divide with one negative number. Build: multiply or divide with two negative numbers. Confidence: choose the correct order in a short expression, including brackets and operations of equal priority.

For multiplication and division of non-zero numbers: same signs give a positive result; different signs give a negative result. Zero multiplied by any number is zero; zero divided by a non-zero number is zero. Never divide by zero. Calculate brackets first, then multiplication/division from left to right, then addition/subtraction from left to right. Powers are outside this set. The sign rule for multiplication/division is not an addition/subtraction rule.

Seven teaching/question slides in the teaching deck; six answer sections in the single separate answer deck shared by all topics. S01 is a worked demo; S02–S07 each have an answer partner, labelled S02-A through S07-A. The answer sections below are content specifications for that shared deck, not slides to interleave into the teaching deck. Show them after students have attempted the questions. The four banks provide repeated starters, not one sitting. Students can begin with the suitable thread; the three threads are not GCSE grade labels.

## S01 — Worked demo

Student instruction: **Follow each calculation. Explain what each sign means.**

| Step | Start | Build | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question | (−4) × 3 | (−24) ÷ (−6) | −3 + 4 × 2 |
| Choose the First Calculation | One multiplication: (−4) × 3. | One division: (−24) ÷ (−6). | Multiply 4 × 2 before adding. |
| Determine Its Sign | Different signs: negative. | Same signs: positive. | 4 × 2 is positive. |
| Complete the Calculation | 4 × 3 = 12, so (−4) × 3 = −12. | 24 ÷ 6 = 4, so (−24) ÷ (−6) = 4. | 4 × 2 = 8; −3 + 8 = 5. |
| Check the Answer | −12 ÷ 3 = −4. Answer: −12. | 4 × (−6) = −24. Answer: 4. | 8 ÷ 2 = 4; 5 − 8 = −3. Answer: 5. |

Diagram specification: Start: editable number line from −12 to 0 with three leftward jumps of 4 from 0 to −12, labelled “3 groups of −4”. Build: an editable inverse check beside the quotient: “4 × (−6) = −24”, with the 4 linked to the answer above the division. Confidence: box 4 × 2 in −3 + 4 × 2; link it to 8 in the next line −3 + 8 = 5. Keep all original numbers visible. On S02-A, box −3 + 7 inside the brackets before multiplying. On S03-A, underline 24 ÷ (−3) and then show −8 × 2. Do not suggest the two-negative multiplication rule is proved by the one-negative number line.
