# Three-page topic practice coverage

The [live website banks](../website/README.md#teaching-content-and-imports) remain authoritative. The 12 October 2026 additions use `M01/M02/M10-P3-…` IDs and append to existing banks without changing existing question objects or bank revisions. They are newly authored for this website, following the [teaching specifications](TEACHING_SPECIFICATIONS.md) and [question-development skill](../.agents/skills/math-problem-development/SKILL.md), not imported from additional slides.

Each topic has 36 independent, 6 scaffolded and 12 error questions at each of Start, Build and Confidence: three pages of 12, 2 and 4. Existing saved assessments, demonstrations and Practice-set code slots are unchanged. New six-question assessments randomly sample two eligible independent questions per level, with arithmetic shape checks excluding historically mislabelled/easier items. Their questions remain fixed after starting. Original dedicated assessment objects remain preserved as historical content. Added totals: 108 independent, 36 scaffolded and 71 error questions (215 overall), including 48 arithmetic error diagrams. M10 Confidence already contained five error questions, so it receives seven rather than eight.

| Topic | Start additions | Build additions | Confidence additions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lattice multiplication | Two digits × one digit; cell halves, diagonal reading, zeros and carries | Two digits × two digits; multiple cell products and diagonal sums/carries | Mixed 4×3, 3×4 and 4×4 operands; substantial digit products, repeated carries, internal zero case |
| Bus stop division | Exact division without carrying; place-value zeros and quotient alignment | One-digit divisors 6–9 with carrying and remainders | Four-digit dividends with two-digit divisors; exact answers and terminating decimals, retained quotient zeros |
| Equations | One-step addition/subtraction, multiplication/division, reversed equalities, negative and fractional solutions | Two-step equations, signed constants/coefficients, fractional solutions, divided-variable forms | Brackets and variables on both sides; negative coefficients after collecting, negative/fractional solutions |

Error activities vary the misconception. Multiplication covers cell facts, exchanged cell halves, omitted/doubled carry, diagonal arithmetic, misplaced tens, zero products and an appended answer zero. Division covers quotient facts, omitted/replaced zeros, reversed reading, invalid remainders, discarded/rounded remainders, lost/misinterpreted carries and decimal placement. Equations use the existing inverse, balance, coefficient, whole-side, expansion and arithmetic diagnoses. Each offers a question-specific hint and repair, an incorrect answer consistent with the shown error, and a check of the correct answer. Repair-choice positions vary.

Review checks live in [topic-page tests](../tests/topic-pages.test.mjs) and the [bank validator](../website/scripts/validate-bank.mjs): exact coverage, distinct questions within each stage/level, arithmetic answer identities, level structures, correct equation-row substitution, first incorrect rows, consequent incorrect answers, corrected options and error-image existence. Inspect diagrams as teaching content too: mathematical tests cannot prove visual clarity or appropriate scaffolding. Existing questions retain their authored provenance and are not claimed to have been re-reviewed by this addition. In particular, the separately recorded older Confidence multiplication replacements remain deferred.

Navigation and saved-attempt rules live in [UI rules](../docs/UI_UX_RULES.md#topic-question-pages). Legacy active attempts keep their questions, so they can overlap the newly defined page partitions; preserving that work takes precedence over retroactively reassigning it. Adaptive trials can replace remaining questions with another level, while the page retains its original navigation position.
