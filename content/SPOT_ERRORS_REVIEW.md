# Spot-the-errors question clarity review — 1 October 2026

Reviewed all 45 prompts alongside their deliberately incorrect written work, corrections and checks. Criteria: natural context; explicit people/objects and units; clear known quantity and target; no hidden assumptions about capacity, sharing, totals or differences; no wording that tells students the intended error. Correct mathematics and the visual diagnostic task remain aligned.

The initial clarity pass revised 14 prompts and retained 31, without changing their numbers. A subsequent answer-diversity pass varied 12 examples; the correct-answer table below now reflects those current results. See [the answer-pattern review](SPOT_ERRORS_ANSWER_PATTERNS.md) for the latest numerical inventory. The prize question uses short column multiplication (12 × 4 = 48) after the same deliberately truncated division, so its full three-line question and written work fit without reducing the font.
