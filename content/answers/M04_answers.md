| Method | 7 parts; 84 ml ÷ 7 = 12 ml per part; 12 ml × 3 = 36 ml of red paint. | 35 folders represent 7 parts. 35 ÷ 7 = 5 per part; 5 × 4 = 20 notebooks. | The difference is 5 − 2 = 3 parts. 24 ÷ 3 = 8 per part; 8 × 7 = 56 people. |
| If you got… | 48 ml: check whether you used 12 ml × 4. That is white paint; red has 3 parts. | 32 notebooks: check whether you used 35 − (7 − 4). The difference of 3 is in parts, not individual items. | 168 people: check whether you used 24 × 7. The 24 represents 3 parts, not 1; divide by 3 first. |
| Check | Red: 36 ml; White: 48 ml. 36 ml + 48 ml = 84 ml, and 36:48 simplifies to 3:4. | 20:35 simplifies to 4:7. There should be fewer notebooks than folders. | Adults: 16; children: 40. 40 − 16 = 24; 16 + 40 = 56. |

### Worked rows S02

| Row | Thread 1: Guided | Thread 2: Core | Thread 3: Depth |

| Written Method | 84 ml ÷ 7 = 12 ml per part. 12 ml × 3 = 36 ml. | 35 ÷ 7 = 5 per part. 5 × 4 = 20 notebooks. | 24 ÷ 3 = 8 per part. 8 × 7 = 56 people. |
