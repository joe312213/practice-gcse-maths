# M02 — Bus stop division: website specification

Current website behaviour; historical authored teaching sources and question IDs are preserved. See the [teaching index](../../docs/INDEX.md#teaching-content) for provenance and source material.

## Question wording

Default `questionPrefix`: **Work out the division**. Show it immediately above the question in the answering area. Contextual applications override this with **Solve the problem**. Keep the input label **Answer**; provide required units separately.

## Sensible quick checks

No calculator needed. Estimate using nearby easy multiples of the divisor, and check the quotient has a sensible place value. For a remainder answer, it must be non-negative and smaller than the divisor. When manageable mentally, multiply the quotient by the divisor and add the remainder (145 ÷ 6 gives 24 r 1: 24 × 6 + 1 = 145). Use an estimate instead of a burdensome inverse calculation for long decimal answers.
