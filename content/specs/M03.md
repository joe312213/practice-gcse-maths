## Challenge and pages

- **Start:** select multiplication or exact division for equal groups, sharing, length and money. An occasional explicit metre-to-centimetre error develops unit checking.
- **Build:** distinguish full packs, enough vehicles/containers, leftover material and exact money shares; vary objectives so division does not always mean rounding the same way. Rates × time retain worthwhile written arithmetic.
- **Confidence:** link two operations, including interpreting a remainder before a cost calculation, an extra charge/discount, stock used, repeated groups and sharing a total cost. Challenge comes from decisions as well as operands.

Three full pages per stage/level: 12 independent, 2 scaffolded, 4 error questions. Bank total: 171 plus three recaps. Retain 72 historical independent questions, six scaffolded questions, three demonstrations and nine reviewed errors. Add 36 independent, 12 scaffolded and 27 errors. New contexts/values must remain unambiguous and numerically accessible without a calculator.

Hints prompt attention to quantities and the requested result; do not name a written method before students choose the operation. Feedback follows the historical answers HTML: **answer with units → worked method → specific “If you got…” misconception → check**. Keep quantity roles consistent: the old HTML swaps hours/rate in some illustrative captions although the products are unchanged; the saved question wording is authoritative. Distinguish approximate ballpark checks from exact inverse checks. Use the existing arithmetic topics for detailed lattice/bus-stop instruction; application feedback shows the linked calculation and interpretation.

## Method animation

Follow the [shared contract](../../docs/METHOD_ANIMATIONS.md). Inspect the chosen operands, then reveal their result. For remainder problems, show the identity total = full groups × group size + remainder; next highlight the decision to retain the quotient, take the remainder or add one group. Reveal that interpreted result before using it in the next calculation. Never show a cost based on unrounded group requirements. Completed working stays visible; pause/seek/replay reproduce the same input/result phases. Finish with the inverse/context check and units. A word problem need not have an ornamental diagram; its explicit, highlighted calculation sequence is the teaching visual.
