## Website delivery

Use all five learning stages, a random six-question assessment (two per level), and three full pages per level of independent/scaffolded/error practice (12/2/4 questions per page). Provide specific hints, full answers, inverse/contextual checks and plausible incorrect working with one correct repair. Use stable IDs and normal progress storage.

Bank/route metadata marks Draft. An outlined, theme-compatible stamp overlays the card and topic introduction without changing their dimensions or obscuring headings/controls. Existing topics stay unmarked.

N2 includes proper/improper fractions and mixed numbers, N3 inverses, N4 common factors/multiples and N8 exact fractions. Multiplication/division of fractions remain separate later modules.

## Challenge progression

- Start: common denominators, both operations, simplification and results at/above one.
- Build: denominators where one is a multiple of the other; use equivalent fractions.
- Confidence: unrelated denominators (including shared factors) and mixed numbers.

Keep questions numerical and scaffolded subtraction non-negative. Model mixed-to-improper conversion before testing it. Diagrams must use equal-sized wholes. Diagnose combining denominators, changing the operation and simplifying only the numerator. Accept equivalent fractions, exact decimals and mixed numbers; simplification is taught but has no separate mark in this draft.

## Method animation

Inspect each mixed number then reveal its improper fraction. Highlight denominators, then reveal both equivalent fractions together, changing numerator and denominator consistently. Equal-whole strips show their common unit. Inspect the two numerators and operation, then reveal the new numerator over the unchanged denominator. Finally inspect that result and reveal its simplest equivalent form. Retain completed lines and end with the inverse operation using the original second operand.

## Verification and review

Follow the [method animation contract](../../docs/METHOD_ANIMATIONS.md). Inspect/write frames must reproduce the same state on pause/seek/replay/speed changes and reduced motion. Static feedback keeps full working. Independent tests recalculate answers from operands and check coverage, exact marking and plausible wrong answers. Browser checks cover overflow, stamps, navigation, playback and submission. Teacher review of wording, variety and diagrams is required before removing Draft.
