# Sixth-stage and combined problem solving

Authorised 12 October 2026. Existing stage IDs stay unchanged; `problem` is sixth for method topics. Dedicated problem-solving topics (currently M03) finish at Independent practice, with five stages and How to solve tips in that final stage; retained problem banks still serve Practice sets/combined practice. Each source topic has three six-question pages at Start/Build/Confidence, with stable WEB-PS IDs, units, authored plans, exact answers, likely mistakes and appropriate estimates/inverse checks. Application content follows numerical methods and does not replace them.

Earlier arithmetic Start uses one clear operation or percentage operator; Build links two steps; Confidence links three or four. Later ratio/reverse-percentage topics require their prerequisite part/whole reasoning even at Start. All application problems stay within one to four arithmetic operations, including finding matching ratio parts or the percentage represented. Contexts must ask students to choose their operations, retain intermediate results and interpret the answer. Mixed practice must preserve the source topic of every question. New authored content is Draft pending teacher review even when its host method topic is already accepted.

`How to solve tips` is a reveal/hide teaching section shared by ordinary and combined problem pages. Steps describe planning, intermediate quantities and checking; the worked example matches the selected level. Opening it marks the active question assisted, and subsequent questions remain assisted while it is open. Closing it does not erase assistance already recorded. Playback reuses ShowDemo and calculation working.

Combined practice is reached through the Practice-set builder and a configurable Combined problems topic page. Learners choose at least two topics and a level. Six problem questions or ten mixed-revision questions are interleaved across source topics, with priority based on the existing revision evidence. Mixed pages record outcomes in source-topic tracks without cross-topic automatic promotion. Page question IDs remain fixed when resuming. Codes preserve the existing 54-bit contract; combined code topic 31 is reserved. A configured topic-page URL retains selected topics in its `topics` query; a bare combined code uses all available topics.

Original teaching banks and saved schema-2 attempts remain authoritative. Do not regenerate existing banks from temporary authoring scripts. Foundation scope uses the [DfE subject content](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gcse-mathematics-subject-content-and-assessment-objectives); no exam board is assumed.
