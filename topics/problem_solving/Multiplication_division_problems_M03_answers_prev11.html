M03-SE — Spot the errors: corrections

Start

1. 14 boxes hold 8 pens each. How many pens altogether?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: Equal groups require 14 × 8 = 112 pens.

Correct working

Check: Ballpark: 10 × 8 = 80 and 20 × 8 = 160. Expect between 80 and 160 pens.

2. 96 stickers are shared equally among 4 pupils. How many per pupil?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: Carry the 1: 16 ÷ 4 = 4. Answer: 24 stickers each.

Correct working

Check: 24 × 4 = 96.

3. Cut 3 m of ribbon into 6 equal pieces. Find each length in cm.

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: 3 m = 300 cm; 300 ÷ 6 = 50 cm.

Correct working

Check: 6 × 50 cm = 300 cm = 3 m.

Build

1. Pack 166 eggs in boxes of 6. How many full boxes?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: 166 ÷ 6 = 27 r 4. Only complete boxes count: 27 full boxes, with 4 eggs left.

Correct working

Check: 27 × 6 + 4 = 166; 28 × 6 = 168 eggs, more than available.

2. 32 campers need tents. Each tent sleeps 5. How many tents are needed?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: Six tents sleep only 30 campers. The remaining 2 campers need another tent: 7 tents.

Correct working

Check: 6 × 5 = 30 places is insufficient; 7 × 5 = 35 places.

3. Share a £156 bill equally among 8 friends. How much each?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: 156 ÷ 8 = 19 r 4. The remaining £4 shared among 8 is £0.50 each, not £0.04. Each pays £19.50.

Correct working

Check: Ballpark: 8 × £20 = £160, slightly above £156. Each share should be just below £20.

Confidence

1. Tables seat 6 and cost £12 to hire. Find the cost to seat 94 guests.

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: All 94 guests need seats. 94 ÷ 6 = 15 r 4, so hire 16 tables. 16 × £12 = £192.

Correct working

Check: 15 tables seat only 90; 16 seat 96.

2. 24 packs hold 15 drinks each. 87 are served. How many are left?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: There are 24 × 15 = 360 drinks. Serving 87 reduces the stock: 360 − 87 = 273 drinks left.

Correct working

Check: 273 + 87 = 360.

3. Four organisers split the cost of 18 prizes equally. Each prize costs £12. How much does each pay?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: The organisers share the cost equally, not just the cost of four whole prizes each. All 18 prizes cost 18 × £12 = £216. £216 ÷ 4 = £54 per organiser. Discarding the remainder from 18 ÷ 4 leaves two prizes unpaid for.

Correct working

Check: 4 × £54 = £216, which pays for all 18 prizes at £12 each.
