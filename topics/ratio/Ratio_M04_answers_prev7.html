M04 — Ratio: sharing and missing amounts

M04-IA — Initial assessment

1. Share £35 between A and B in the ratio 2:5. How much does A receive?

Answer: £10
7 parts = £35; 1 part = £5; A receives 2 × £5.

2. Red and blue counters are in the ratio 3:4. There are 12 red counters. How many blue counters are there?

Answer: 16 blue counters
3 parts = 12; 1 part = 4; blue = 4 × 4.

3. Two ribbons have lengths in the ratio 2:7. The longer is 25 cm longer than the shorter. Find the shorter length.

Answer: 10 cm
5 extra parts = 25 cm; 1 part = 5 cm; shorter = 2 × 5 cm.

4. Adults and children are in the ratio 5:8. There are 39 people altogether. How many more children than adults are there?

Answer: 9 more children
13 parts = 39; 1 part = 3; difference = (8 − 5) × 3.

M04-SE — Spot the errors: corrections

Start

1. Share £35 between A and B in ratio 2:5. How much does A get?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: A has 2 parts: 2 × £5 = £10; B gets £25.

Correct working

Check: 10:25 = 2:5; £10 + £25 = £35.

2. Red:blue = 3:4. There are 12 red counters. How many blue?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: The 12 counters are the red amount, not the total. Divide by the 3 red parts: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 per part. Blue = 4 × 4 = 16 counters. The shown solution also discards a remainder.

Correct working

Check: 12:16 = 3:4.

3. Share 12 sweets between A and B in ratio 1:3. How many does B get?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: B has 3 parts, each worth 3 sweets. Multiply: B = 3 × 3 = 9 sweets, rather than adding 3 + 3.

Correct working

Check: A gets 3 sweets and B gets 9; 3 + 9 = 12 and 3:9 = 1:3.

Build

1. A and B share counters in ratio 2:7. B has 30 more than A. How many does A have?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: The difference is 5 parts, so one part is 30 ÷ 5 = 6 counters. A has 2 parts: 2 × 6 = 12 counters, rather than 2 + 6.

Correct working

Check: B has 7 × 6 = 42 counters; 42 − 12 = 30 more than A.

2. Red:blue = 4:3. There are 15 blue counters. How many red counters?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: Blue has 3 parts: 15 ÷ 3 = 5; red = 4 × 5 = 20.

Correct working

Check: 20:15 = 4:3.

3. A and B share 56 counters in ratio 3:5. How many more does B get than A?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: A gets 21 counters and B gets 35. Find how many more B gets by subtracting: 35 − 21 = 14 counters, not adding the two amounts.

Correct working

Check: (5 − 3) × 7 = 14; 21 + 35 = 56 checks the total.

Confidence

1. A group has 65 people. Adults:children = 5:8. How many more children than adults?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: There are 25 adults and 40 children. Compare the numbers of people: 40 − 25 = 15 more children than adults. The difference of 3 ratio parts represents 3 × 5 = 15 people.

Correct working

Check: 25 + 40 = 65; 40 − 25 = 15.

2. Two ribbon lengths are in ratio 3:8 and differ by 35 cm. Find their total length.

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: The requested total is 21 + 56 = 77 cm.

Correct working

Check: 77 ÷ 11 = 7 cm per part; difference 5 × 7 = 35 cm.

3. A and B have tokens in ratio 7:4. A has 42. How many tokens in total?

If you got… (incorrect work)

Answer / Method: Add the amounts, not the ratio part count: 42 + 24 = 66 tokens.

Correct working

Check: 11 × 6 = 66.
