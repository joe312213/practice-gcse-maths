About Maths practice

Author and purpose

Created by Joe Hudson to help Foundation GCSE Maths students practise accurate methods and build confidence through short, regular activities.

Credits

The theme palettes and controls are adapted from Joe Hudson’s T-Level starters site. Its source code also informed the approach to practice, feedback and saved progress.

How it is developed

This is an agent-augmented development project: Joe sets the teaching direction and reviews the material, with OpenAI Codex assisting with implementation, content preparation and checks. Automated maths and behaviour tests are used alongside browser and visual checks.

The site is still being developed. Those checks support review; they do not imply that every topic or feature is finished.

Your progress

Names and progress are stored in this browser on this device. They are not online accounts and are not synchronised across devices.
