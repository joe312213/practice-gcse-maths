Counting and rules

White receives komi to balance Black’s first move. Count at any point, or pass twice to start counting. Marked dead groups are faded; GNU Go predictions do not override your agreed dead groups. Area counting uses your agreed dead groups. Suicide and moves repeating an earlier board position are forbidden. Undo and Redo review moves and passes with clocks paused.
